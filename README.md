# Industrial Management System

## 📌 Overview

The Industrial Management System is a web-based application designed to support industrial and organisational operations by managing employees, payroll, resource allocation, costing, and reporting.

This system was developed based on practical industry requirements and supported by research into industrial management principles such as cost control, resource optimisation, and performance measurement.

The goal of this project was to build a structured, data-driven solution that assists management in making informed operational decisions.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The system was designed to:

- Centralise employee and payroll management
- Track organisational resources and associated costs
- Monitor operational efficiency
- Generate analytical reports for management review
- Reduce manual administrative workload

---

## 🧠 Research & Design Foundation

Before implementation, industry best practices were reviewed to understand:

- Workforce management structures
- Payroll processing workflows
- Resource allocation strategies
- Cost analysis models
- Performance reporting frameworks

Concepts from industrial management and operations management literature (e.g., production economics, productivity tracking, and performance measurement systems) influenced the database structure, system modules, and reporting logic.

This ensured the system design aligned with real-world operational needs rather than being purely technical.

---

## 💡 Key Features

### 👥 Employee Management
- Add, update, and manage employee records
- Store department and role information
- Maintain employee data securely

### 💰 Payroll Management
- Payroll calculation based on attendance or defined rules
- Store payroll history
- Generate payroll summaries

### 🏭 Resource & Cost Management
- Track organisational resources
- Allocate resources to departments or teams
- Monitor cost implications

### 📊 Reporting & Analytics
- Department-wise cost breakdown
- Attendance tracking reports
- Payroll summaries
- Operational data for management decision-making

---

## 🛠 Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- **Backend:** Node.js
- **Architecture:** MVC (Model-View-Controller)
- **Database:** Structured relational database

The MVC architecture ensures separation of concerns, maintainability, and scalability of the application.
